Hello, I'm Krishna Patel 👋

I am a Product Designer with a Strong Visual Design

My design journey began in graphic design, where I learned the power of visual storytelling, composition, and clarity. Over time, I realized I wanted to go beyond aesthetics, to understand why users behave the way they do and how design can solve real problems.

This curiosity led me to pursue formal studies in UI/UX design, where I deepened my skills in UX thinking, user research, interaction design, and usability. Today, with 4+ years of experience in the design field, I combine strong visual craft with human-centered design principles to create intuitive, scalable, and meaningful digital products.

I enjoy transforming complex ideas into clear, purposeful experiences that align user needs with business goals.
